Population, household and structure totals are calculated by summing the corresponding CSV values for the filtered point records. Confirm that these values are compound-level counts and are not repeated EA totals before interpreting national totals.

Methodology

Census point records are mapped using the supplied latitude and longitude fields. Enumeration-area polygons are displayed from the GeoJSON boundary file. Administrative selections filter boundary features and census points spatially. Population, household and structure summaries are calculated from the selected point records.

The dashboard does not infer missing coordinates, redistribute population, or estimate unobserved households. Results depend on the completeness and accuracy of the uploaded source data.
