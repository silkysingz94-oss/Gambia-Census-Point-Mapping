Methodology

Census points are mapped using the longitude and latitude fields in CensusPoints.csv. Enumeration Area boundaries are read from Census_EAs_Geo.json. Administrative filters use attributes available in the boundary file. Population, household and structure statistics are calculated from the mapped CSV fields. Grid concentration is an exploratory spatial summary.
