Methodology and interpretation

Getis-Ord Gi*: evaluates whether a cell and its neighbouring cells have unusually high or low values. The 1 km grid includes zero-value cells within the selected analysis extent. A positive z-score indicates clustering of high values; a negative z-score indicates clustering of low values. The selected confidence threshold is applied to the z-score.

Global Moran's I: measures overall spatial autocorrelation of grid values. Permutation p-values are estimated by randomly reallocating values among grid cells.

KDE: the density layer is a smoothed concentration surface, not a significance test.

Important: results depend on coordinate quality, grid origin, study-area boundary and spatial weights. Grid cells are created over the selected extent's bounding rectangle. Where a precise study-area polygon is unavailable, edge cells may extend outside the administrative boundary. Gi* normal-approximation p-values are not multiple-testing corrected.
