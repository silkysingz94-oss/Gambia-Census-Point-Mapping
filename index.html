Methodology and limitations

Getis-Ord Gi*: Local spatial concentration of high and low grid values. Z-scores use a normal approximation; p-values are adjusted using Benjamini-Hochberg FDR.

Global Moran's I: Measures overall spatial autocorrelation using grid adjacency and permutation inference.

Local Moran's I (LISA): Identifies High-High, Low-Low, High-Low and Low-High local patterns. Quadrant labels are exploratory and are not presented as locally significant without local permutation tests.

Gini coefficient: Measures inequality in the distribution of the selected variable.

Pearson and Spearman: Measure linear and rank correlations using pairwise complete records.

Nearest neighbour: Compares observed mean nearest-point distance with expected distance under complete spatial randomness.

Spatial outliers: Identifies grid cells with values at least two standard deviations from the mean of adjacent cells. This is exploratory screening, not a formal significance test.

KDE: Displays smoothed spatial concentration; it is not a significance test.

DBSCAN: Groups point locations by distance and minimum number of points.

Grid limitation: Grid cells cover the selected points' bounding rectangle, not necessarily the exact administrative boundary. The local equirectangular projection is approximate. Review CRS, boundary coverage and data quality before formal publication.
